# Varun Basnett - Personal Portfolio & Indie Game Sanctum

A responsive, high-performance personal portfolio website for **Varun Basnett**, a **B.Tech student**, software engineer, and passionate indie gamer on Steam.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

### 1. 🎓 B.Tech & Engineering Profile
- **Hero Section**: High-impact introduction with an animated typing tagline, quick metrics, and an engineer avatar badge.
- **About Me**: Narrative exploring the synergy between computer science fundamentals, software engineering, and game design principles. Includes an interactive JSON profile card.
- **Personal Skill Highlights**:
  - Filterable skill cards across **Languages**, **Web & Frontend**, **CS Fundamentals**, **Tools & DevOps**, and **Game Logic & Systems**.
  - Animated skill meters that smoothly fill upon scrolling into view, paired with live percentage counters.
- **Academic & Passion Projects**: 4 featured projects showcasing full-stack development, algorithms, simulations, and mechanics.
- **Contact & Socials**: Interactive message form, email copy button with instant clipboard feedback, and links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Steam, and Discord.

---

### 2. 🎮 Steam Indie Games Sanctums
Dedicated, independently themed zones for Varun's favorite Steam indie games, featuring official transparent PNG logos, custom badges, and interactive simulations:

#### ⚔️ Valheim (The 10th Norse Realm)
- **Visuals**: Official Valheim logo, Norse shield rune PNG badge, and screenshot.
- **Campfire Embers**: Procedural HTML5 canvas rendering floating fire embers.
- **Interactive Runes**: Hovering over Elder Futhark runes (`ᚠ ᚢ ᚦ ᚨ ᚱ ᛉ ᛟ`) deciphers their Norse meanings.
- **Mead Hall Feasting Station**: Click foods (*Lox Meat Pie*, *Serpent Stew*, *Salad*, *Mead*) to watch Health and Stamina bars surge dynamically.
- **Defeated Boss Trophy Wall**: Badges for Eikthyr, The Elder, Bonemass, Moder, Yagluth, and The Queen.
- **Gjallarhorn Audio**: Synthesizes a deep resonant Norse war horn using the Web Audio API.

#### 🗡️ Hollow Knight (Hallownest)
- **Visuals**: Official Hollow Knight logo, pure nail mask PNG badge, and Hallownest screenshot.
- **Lumafly Swarm**: Ambient canvas rendering luminous, pulsing lumafly particles.
- **Interactive Nail Slash**: Clicking or tapping the combat arena unleashes an animated curved nail slash with shockwave and a metallic "SHING!" sound effect.
- **Soul Vessel & Masks**: Cast *Vengeful Spirit*, *Focus* to heal masks, and collect Geo.
- **Charms Build**: Showcases *Mark of Pride*, *Quick Slash*, *Shaman Stone*, and *Wayward Compass*.

#### 🪡 Hollow Knight: Silksong (Pharloom)
- **Visuals**: Official Silksong logo, Hornet needle/spool PNG badge, and Pharloom screenshot.
- **Live Silk Ribbon Canvas**: Move your mouse or drag your finger across the canvas to weave glowing crimson and golden silk strings.
- **Silk Spool Meter**: Animate Hornet's spool replenishment, needle lunges, and silk binds.
- **Pharloom Citadel Chime**: Resonates harmonic bronze bell chimes.
- **Rosary Bead Stringing**: Interactive counter.

#### ☣️ Warhammer: Vermintide 2 (The End Times)
- **Visuals**: Official Vermintide 2 logo, Skaven Warpstone skull PNG badge, and horde screenshot.
- **Warpstone Embers**: Radioactive toxic neon green smoke and spark particles.
- **Horde Cleave Simulator**: Click the arena to cleave incoming Skaven clanrats with blood flashes, floating critical damage numbers, and an escalating kill counter.
- **Ubersreik 5 Careers**: Witch Hunter Captain, Ironbreaker, and Grail Knight.

### 3. 🔊 Procedural Web Audio API
- Fully client-side sound effects—no external audio files needed (eliminates CORS and broken asset issues).
- Synthesizes UI clicks, Norse war horns, metallic nail slashes, silk thread zips, and heavy melee cleaves.
- Sound can be toggled on/off at any time using the floating button in the bottom-left corner.

---

### 4. 📱 Responsive & Accessible Design
- Smooth mobile navigation drawer with a hamburger menu.
- Reading progress bar at the very top of the viewport.
- Circular progress "Back to Top" floating button.
- Custom desktop glowing cursor follower (automatically disabled on touch screens).
